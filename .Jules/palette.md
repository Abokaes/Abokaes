## 2026-07-07 - Structural accessibility in minimalist repositories
**Learning:** In projects where documentation is the primary interface, UX is driven by readability and semantic structure. Missing H1 headings (MD041) and improper line lengths (MD013) impede screen readers and cognitive accessibility.
**Action:** Always use linting tools like `markdownlint` to identify and fix structural issues in Markdown files, and avoid identity injection when polishing user-facing profiles.
