## 2026-07-02 - Artifact Cleanliness in Minimalist Repos
**Learning:** In repositories where the README is the primary interface (like Profile READMEs), including temporary build artifacts like `README.html` or verification screenshots in the final submission constitutes clutter and signals an unpolished development process.
**Action:** Explicitly include a cleanup step in the plan to remove all non-source artifacts generated during the verification workflow before calling submit.
